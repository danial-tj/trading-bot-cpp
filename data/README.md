# Data provenance

`daily_demo.csv`: 360 fictional daily weekday OHLCV bars beginning January 2, 2024. Generated by `scripts/generate_daily_demo.py` from deterministic sine waves and a small drift. No network data, provider or random seed.

`opening_demo.csv`: 38,025 fictional two-minute weekday bars January–September 2024, generated by `scripts/generate_vwap_demo.py`. Full fixed 09:30–16:00 sessions supply VWAP/EMA/weekly/monthly warmup. Some weekdays are actual market holidays; this fixture deliberately does not represent an exchange calendar. Rising and falling regimes demonstrate both long setups and unexecuted short opportunities.

These generated numbers contain no third-party market data. They are bundled as software test fixtures under the project's terms; no external market-data license is implied or required. They are unsuitable evidence of real historical profitability.

`sample_data.csv` is a pre-existing small fixture retained for compatibility/history. It contains January 1, 2023 minute timestamps (a Sunday), and is not used as the new daily demo. Its historical provider/source was not established; it is not represented as real market data.

The CLI accepts your own validated CSV. Fields must be `timestamp,open,high,low,close,volume`, finite positive consistent OHLC, nonnegative volume, and strictly increasing unique canonical timestamps. Opening VWAP requires full intraday regular sessions at the configured bar interval, exchange-local clock timestamps, and sufficient prior completed weeks/months. The browser accepts only bundled datasets.
