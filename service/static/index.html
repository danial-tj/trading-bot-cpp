        From your TradingView chart
        1. Open the symbol you want to test and choose its candle interval, such as 2 minutes.
        2. Load the earlier history you need. The export includes only chart data that has loaded.
        3. In the upper toolbar menu, choose Download chart data… and save the CSV.
        4. Import it here with the same symbol, interval and price currency.
        TradingView export instructions ↗
        File contents
        Use these six columns, with the oldest candle first.
        timestamp,open,high,low,close,volume
        Intraday timestamps mark the start of each candle:
        2026-01-05 09:30:00
        Daily candles use:
        2026-01-05
        Prices and volume must be numeric. Duplicate dates, invalid prices and candles that do not match the chosen interval are rejected.
        For Opening VWAP
        Use intraday candles with complete sessions and several months of prior history for the weekly and monthly filters. Daily data works with SMA, EMA and RSI studies.
        Session times are fixed. Holidays, early closes and corporate actions are not corrected automatically.
        Import checks the file’s structure. It does not independently verify the provider or prices.
